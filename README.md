# DrugSafe AI v3.6

DrugSafe AI is a clinical medication-safety and dose decision-support prototype focused on patient-specific renal/hepatic assessment, dialysis context, interaction and safety warnings, therapeutic drug monitoring, and source traceability.

## Use DrugSafe AI

**Online demo:** https://nuruzade052.github.io/-drugsafe-ai/

**Windows Setup:** https://github.com/nuruzade052/-drugsafe-ai/releases/download/v3.6.0/DrugSafe-AI-Setup-3.6.0-x64.exe

**Windows Portable:** https://github.com/nuruzade052/-drugsafe-ai/releases/download/v3.6.0/DrugSafe-AI-Portable-3.6.0-x64.exe

## Main capabilities

- Patient-specific renal dose assessment
- Hepatic assessment with drug-specific rules
- IHD / CRRT / dialysis context
- Drug interaction and duplicate-therapy review
- Safety Engine: QT prolongation, hyperkalemia, nephrotoxicity, serotonin syndrome, bleeding risk
- Therapeutic drug monitoring and laboratory follow-up suggestions
- Source and rule traceability
- Validation Mode for software regression scenarios

## Important clinical notice

DrugSafe AI is a clinical decision-support prototype. It is not a prescribing, order-entry, or autonomous treatment system. Final medication decisions must be verified against the patient's current clinical condition, official product information, institutional protocols, and appropriate specialist or clinical pharmacy input.
